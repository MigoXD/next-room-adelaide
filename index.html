How it would work

Offer an item

Tell the coordinator what you own and when you’re leaving. The pilot would accept clean, portable, non-electrical items.

Agree on a time

Pick one of two pickup times. If someone doesn’t confirm, the coordinator would offer the item to the next person on the waitlist.

Collect it for free

Pick up in an approved common area. Temporary storage would need building approval and a clear collection deadline.
